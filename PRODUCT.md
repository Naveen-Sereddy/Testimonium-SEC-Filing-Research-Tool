# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Financial analysts, researchers, and recruiters/hiring managers evaluating this as a portfolio piece. Primary users upload an SEC filing (10-K/10-Q) and ask natural-language questions to get page-cited answers without manually reading hundreds of pages. Context: focused desktop work, often referencing the source document side-by-side, sometimes on mobile for a quick check.

## Product Purpose

A RAG-powered financial document assistant: upload a filing, ask questions, get answers grounded in the actual text with page-level citations back to source excerpts. Success = the chat experience feels as trustworthy and fast as a modern AI product, so a recruiter reviewing it as a portfolio project immediately reads it as production-grade rather than a weekend prototype.

## Brand Personality

Serious, precise, fast. References named directly: ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Linear, Vercel, Notion AI — take inspiration from their restraint and speed, not their exact visual language.

## Anti-references

Generic internal admin/CRUD tool: default component-library styling, thin visual hierarchy, sluggish or clunky interactions, dated chat-bubble conventions.

## Design Principles

- Trust through grounding — every claim traces to a visible citation; the UI should make that traceability effortless, not buried.
- Speed reads as intelligence — streaming and transitions should feel instant, never artificially throttled.
- Conversation over interface — the composer and message flow should recede so the content (the filing, the answer) stays the focus.
- Restraint over decoration — one accent color, purposeful motion, no glassmorphism/gradient-text/card-pile clichés.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA baseline: 4.5:1 body text contrast, visible keyboard focus rings on every interactive element, ARIA labels on icon-only buttons, `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all animation, minimum 44px touch targets on mobile.
