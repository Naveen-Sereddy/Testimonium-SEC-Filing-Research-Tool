# Demo filing provenance

`public/demo/sample-10k.pdf` is a sample regulatory filing used to exercise the upload, retrieval, citation, table, and refusal flows.

- **Company:** Heron Therapeutics, Inc.
- **Filing:** Form 10-K
- **Fiscal year ended:** December 31, 2025
- **Source:** U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission filing, as identified on the document's cover page. The exact EDGAR accession URL is not recorded in this repository.
- **Purpose:** Demonstration and local evaluation data only; it is not client data.

The repository's MIT license applies to the project code. It does not grant rights to reuse the filing or any other third-party content; users should verify the source document's applicable terms independently.
